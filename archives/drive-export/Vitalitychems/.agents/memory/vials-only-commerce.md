---
name: Vials-only commerce model
description: Durable catalog cost basis and checkout safeguards for the current research-supply business model.
---
Rule: the active catalog is vial-only. Standard supplier cost is the uploaded sheet's `1 KIT (10)` total divided by ten; larger kit columns are bulk projections. Unknown costs remain unknown, never zero.

**Why:** the owner confirmed they normally order ten bottles at a time. Treating another column as standard cost or coercing unreadable/missing costs to zero would distort margin, ROI, wholesale, and investor outputs.

**How to apply:** keep removed capsules, liquids, and SARMs unavailable through authoritative checkout lookup as well as UI filtering. Preserve historical order snapshots. Any new vial must have a confirmed sheet cost or an explicit cost-missing state.

Rule: referrals require a verified signed-in identity and settle only after payment; first paid orders require an audited staff research-use approval before shipping.

**Why:** email-only guest identity permits self/referral abuse, while a recorded-but-unenforced review does not provide the represented operational safeguard.

**How to apply:** retain transactional canonical-identity checks, self/repeat-use prevention, paid-settlement credit award, and the shipping approval predicate when changing checkout, settlement, referrals, or admin orders.

Rule: `vitalitychem.com` (singular) is the primary canonical domain.

**Why:** the owner replaced the earlier domain choice with a domain purchased through Replit.

**How to apply:** use it for canonical, sitemap, auth/trusted origins, checkout returns, affiliate links, and sender-domain setup. A Replit-purchased domain attaches and receives managed TLS only after publishing; Stripe's managed production webhook cannot be verified before then.

Rule: public customer endorsements must come from real, consented purchasers and remain limited to service, ordering, documentation, packaging, and fulfillment—not human or animal outcomes.

**Why:** describing an invented quote as an “alleged customer” statement is still deceptive, and health/outcome testimonials can create intended-use and advertising risk.

**How to apply:** do not seed testimonial copy. Tie future review submissions to completed orders, moderate prohibited claims, support correction/removal, and emit review schema only for reviews visibly published on the site.
