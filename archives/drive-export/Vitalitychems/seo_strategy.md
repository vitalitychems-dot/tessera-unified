# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public storefront, catalog, product, testing/COA, wholesale, informational, policy, and newsletter pages for Vitality Chems.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated customer account, checkout/order flows, API endpoints, and staff admin pages.
- Workspace template artifacts that are not used by the storefront.

## Target audience
- Adults seeking research peptides and related laboratory-use products with transparent testing documentation.

## Primary keywords
- Research peptides
- Peptides for research
- Peptide testing and COA documentation
- Wholesale research peptides

## Crawler assumptions
- Public pages should be server-rendered and discoverable by search, social-preview, and AI crawlers.
- Primary canonical domain is https://vitalitychems.com (with an “s”); legacy vitalitychem.com variants redirect to it.
