# Vitality Supply compliance language audit

**Audit date:** 2026-09-15  
**Scope:** Public storefront language, research-use notices, checkout attestations,
returns, shipping/fulfillment statements, subscriptions, wholesale, referrals, and
transactional copy in `artifacts/vitality-supply`. This document is not legal advice.
It is an implementation record for review by qualified US and Illinois counsel.

## Decisions implemented

1. **RUO notices are repeated, specific, and non-evasive.** Public copy now says
   products are offered solely for qualified laboratory, in-vitro, and analytical
   work; prohibits human and animal use; and avoids administration, ingestion,
   dosing, diagnosis, treatment, efficacy, therapeutic, weight, bodybuilding, and
   anti-aging claims. Chemistry, CAS, molecular formula/weight, HPLC, LC-MS, lot,
   COA, handling, and catalog-quantity information remain available where supplied.
2. **RUO is not presented as a safe harbor.** The site visibly states that an RUO
   label alone does not establish regulatory legality and that it does not guarantee
   the legality of a sale, possession, shipment, or use. Purchasers are told to
   independently determine applicable law, permits, institutional rules,
   destination restrictions, and payment-provider requirements.
3. **No pharmacy, prescription, compounding, or FDA-approval suggestion.** Public
   language describes a chemical supplier and does not offer prescribing,
   dispensing, pharmacy, or compounding services. It does not represent products as
   FDA-approved, cleared, or authorized.
4. **Purchaser and order controls are disclosed.** The age gate and checkout require
   an 18+ research-use representation. Research-use review, order holds, refusals,
   cancellations, and payment or destination review are disclosed as discretionary
   controls and are not described as a legal certification.
5. **Commercial copy is bounded.** Returns exclude opened materials and research
   outcome/protocol disputes. Subscription and wholesale requests are subject to
   review and do not guarantee stock, delivery, legality, or protocol suitability.
   Referral/affiliate copy prohibits regulated claims and requires compliance with
   applicable advertising and platform rules.
6. **Documentation claims were narrowed.** COA language distinguishes lot-specific
   results from general catalog information and says results apply only to the
   tested sample. Outcome testimonials were replaced with supplier policy
   statements.

## Counsel-required blockers and follow-up

The following remain open and must not be treated as resolved by copy changes:

- Obtain a product-by-product classification and intended-use review, including
  whether any listed compound, blend, diluent, encapsulated material, topical
  preparation, or label creates a drug, device, cosmetic, dietary-supplement,
  controlled-substance, or other regulated-product issue.
- Confirm the supplier's Illinois and federal business, facility, manufacturing,
  testing, storage, shipping, and any wholesale/licensing requirements. Confirm
  whether any pharmacy, wholesale-drug-distributor, manufacturer, or other
  registration is required or should be obtained.
- Review every destination state and country before shipment. Confirm import/export,
  controlled-substance, prescription, age, and carrier restrictions and establish a
  documented refusal/hold workflow.
- Validate that product names, purity specifications, COAs, analytical methods,
  supplier statements, batch identifiers, shipping times, and “same-day dispatch”
  claims are supported by current records. Do not publish a claim that cannot be
  substantiated.
- Have counsel review affiliate, referral, discount, subscription, email, social,
  SEO, schema, and wholesale advertising for implied intended use, endorsements,
  disclosures, and platform/payment-provider rules.
- Confirm returns, refunds, chargebacks, payment methods, customer data retention,
  age verification, and purchaser-attestation practices with counsel and the
  applicable processors.
- Confirm whether research-use labeling, SDS/handling documentation, and any
  required hazard, storage, lot, or recordkeeping language is complete for each SKU.

Until these blockers are resolved, this audit should be read only as a conservative
language and disclosure pass. It is not legal advice, a regulatory opinion, an FDA
determination, or a guarantee that a sale or use is lawful.
